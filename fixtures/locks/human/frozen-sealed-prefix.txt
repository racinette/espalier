  ❄️ marks where a freeze begins: the files it follows are frozen, and so is

  and is never removed, so add a new one instead where the contract admits it
  (`espalier help locks`). A count after a pattern, such as `0+` or `1+`, is the
  fewest matching names that must exist.

  repository-specific purpose.
