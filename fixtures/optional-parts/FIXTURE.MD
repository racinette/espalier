# Optional parts of a name

Pins MATCHING.MD "Alternatives". One migration rule names its steps
`<num><ord>_<table>.<kind>.sql`, and a grants step may add a role after the
kind: `{,.[role=a+]}` is either nothing or a dot and a role. All of a table's
steps, of every kind, are one rule's files, so one grouping judges the set.

`role` is captured only where it is written. The rule's own `lint` reports a
role on a step that is not a grant, and the set reports the gap that step
leaves. Generated guidance explains the empty alternative in the document's
legend, and the map hangs a description below a name too long to share its
line.

Afterwards the stray step is replaced by `0001001_order.indexes.sql`, and the
repository conforms.
