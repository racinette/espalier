# Case styles and repeat ranges

Pins MATCHING.MD "Case styles" and the `(m..n)` repetition under "Masks".
Components are Pascal, hooks camel, routes kebab and jobs snake; constants are
SNAKE and headers KEBAB, the screaming styles. A name in another style falls to
a bare sibling rule where there is one and is undeclared where there is not.
Runs of capitals and digits are allowed, so `HTMLView.tsx` is a component and
`utf-8.ts` a route. Migrations take four or more digits, codes two or three
capital letters followed by up to two digits, and revisions twelve lowercase
hex digits, `h(12)`.

Also pins the legend generated guidance gives each case style and each form of
range.
